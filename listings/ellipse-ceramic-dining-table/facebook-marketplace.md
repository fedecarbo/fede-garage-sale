## Buyer questions you'll get

- **"Is it still available?"** — the standard opener; answer fast, most sales go to whoever replies first.
- **"What's the lowest you'll take?"** — "£200 and it's yours today" holds the line without killing the thread.
- **"Will it fit in my car?"** — top comes off the base and travels flat; a 120 cm disc needs an estate, hatchback with seats down, or a van.
- **"Any damage?"** — whatever you confirmed in the condition line above.
- **"Can you deliver?"** — collection only unless you want to offer otherwise.
- **"Why are you selling?"** — clearing the flat.
- **"Are the chairs included?"** — no, separate: £40 each or £200 for all six.

Ignore anyone who opens by asking for your phone number or offers to send a courier and
pay by bank transfer — both are the standard Marketplace scam patterns. Cash on collection.
